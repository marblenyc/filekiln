Questions this tool gets asked

Where does lorem ipsum come from?

It is scrambled Latin lifted from Cicero's De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum (45 BC), in use as printer's filler since at least the 1500s and standard in typesetting ever since Letraset sheets popularized it.

Why not just use real text as placeholder?

Readable words pull reviewers into reading instead of looking. Nonsense text with natural word-length distribution keeps the review on layout, spacing, and type - which is the point of a draft.

Can I get a specific word count instead of paragraphs?

Generate a paragraph more than you need and trim; the word counter tool on this site will tell you exactly where you stand.

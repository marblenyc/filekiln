Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-30

Your data

The text and files you put into FileKiln tools are processed entirely in your browser by client-side JavaScript. They are not transmitted to, stored on, or readable by our servers. We have no database of user content because no user content ever reaches us.

Analytics and advertising

Pages may include privacy-respecting traffic measurement and may display advertising served by third-party networks such as Google AdSense. Ad networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads; see how Google uses information from partner sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Cookie consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent message (provided by Google's certified consent management platform) is shown before any personalized advertising cookies are used. You can consent, decline, or manage purposes individually; declining shows non-personalized ads. Your choice can be changed at any time via the ad privacy link or your browser's site settings. Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the CCPA, Brazil's LGPD, and Canada's PIPEDA you hold rights of access, correction, and deletion over personal data an operator stores about you - and because FileKiln's tools run entirely in your browser with no accounts and no upload, the site itself holds none. FileKiln is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no information from anyone of any age. If you believe data is being processed unlawfully, you may complain to your local supervisory authority.

Third-party scripts

Some tools load an open-source library (for example, the js-yaml parser) from the jsDelivr content delivery network. CDN requests are standard web requests and do not include the content you type into a tool.

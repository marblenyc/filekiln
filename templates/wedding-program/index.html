Private by construction. The document is assembled by this page's JavaScript as you type. Names, prices, and photos never leave your machine, and the page keeps working with the network unplugged. Printing uses your browser's own print dialog - choose "Save as PDF" there to get a file.

A wedding program is four sections and a lot of nerve-wracking formatting - so this page does the formatting. Type the ceremony moments one per line, add the wedding party, pick one of five palettes, and the program on the right rebuilds as you type, ready to print at Letter or A4 or save as a PDF for the print shop. Like every FileKiln template it runs entirely in your browser: the names of everyone you love stay on your screen, not on a template site's server.

Questions this template gets asked

What goes in a wedding program?

Four things, in order: the header (your names, date, venue), the order of ceremony (processional through recessional, usually 6-10 moments), the wedding party (officiant, parents, attendants), and a short thank-you to your guests. This template holds exactly those four sections and nothing it doesn't need.

Can I change the program's colors?

Yes - pick from five palettes (classic black, sage, blush, navy and gold, terracotta) and the printed program restyles instantly. The palette prints exactly as previewed.

What size does it print at?

Letter or A4 straight from your browser - a classic full-page program. For a folded half-page program, choose two pages per sheet in your print dialog; for heavier stock, save as PDF and hand it to any print shop.

Is my ceremony information uploaded anywhere?

No. The program is assembled by this page's JavaScript as you type, and printing uses your browser's own dialog. Names and details exist only on your screen - the page keeps working with the internet unplugged.

Is this wedding program template really free?

Yes - no account, no email, no watermark. Fill it in, pick a palette, print as many copies as you have guests.

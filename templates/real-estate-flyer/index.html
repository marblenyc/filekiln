Real Estate Flyer Template

Private by construction. The document is assembled by this page's JavaScript as you type. Names, prices, and photos never leave your machine, and the page keeps working with the network unplugged. Printing uses your browser's own print dialog - choose "Save as PDF" there to get a file.

Type the listing, drop in the exterior shot, and the flyer builds itself: photo on top, price band, the beds-baths-square-feet row buyers scan first, a short description, and a two-column feature list. The photo is read by your browser with the File API and drawn straight into the page - it is never uploaded, which also means an unlisted property you are still preparing stays private. Print a stack for the open-house table, or save as PDF for email. The footer carries the standard information-deemed-reliable line and the Equal Housing Opportunity notice.

Questions this template gets asked

What makes a real estate flyer effective?

One strong exterior photo, the price stated plainly, and the three numbers every buyer checks - beds, baths, square feet - visible without reading. Cluttered flyers with ten photos and paragraph soup get put down; this layout is deliberately restrained.

What photo should I use?

The front exterior in good light, landscape orientation. The flyer renders it at roughly 4:3; a phone photo at default resolution prints fine at Letter size. Skip wide-angle distortion - buyers notice, and misrepresentation complaints start with photos.

Do I need the Equal Housing Opportunity notice?

In the US, fair-housing law applies to housing advertising, and the EHO notice is standard practice on marketing materials. This template includes the line by default; your brokerage's compliance rules may require its logo as well.

Can I use this for a rental listing?

Yes - set the price field to the monthly rent (e.g. “$2,150/mo”) and use the features list for lease terms like available date, pet policy, and utilities included.

Can I make a flyer online free without a watermark or sign-up?

Yes. No account, no watermark, no trial. The flyer builds in your browser - including the photo, which is read locally and never uploaded - and prints or saves as PDF from the print dialog.

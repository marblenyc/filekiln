Wedding Day Timeline Template

Build an hour-by-hour wedding day schedule, pick a color palette, and print it from your browser - for the couple, the party, and the vendors. Nothing is uploaded.

Private by construction. The document is assembled by this page's JavaScript as you type. Names, prices, and photos never leave your machine, and the page keeps working with the network unplugged. Printing uses your browser's own print dialog - choose "Save as PDF" there to get a file.

The wedding day timeline is the least romantic document of the whole event and the one that saves it: one page that tells eight different people where to be for twelve straight hours. Type each moment as time | what | where and the schedule rebuilds as you type - then print a copy for the wedding party, the parents, the coordinator, the photographer, and the DJ. The sample schedule is a realistic 5pm-ceremony day you can edit rather than a blank page you have to invent.

Questions this template gets asked

What times should a wedding day timeline include?

Work backward from the ceremony. A typical 5pm ceremony day: hair and makeup from 11am (about 45-60 minutes per person plus a buffer), photographer arrives 2:30, first look 3:30, guests seated 4:45, ceremony 5:00, cocktail hour 5:30, reception entrance 6:30, dinner 7:00, toasts 7:45, first dance 8:15, cake 9:00, last dance 10:45, send-off 11:00.

Who should get a copy of the timeline?

Everyone who has to be somewhere on time: the wedding party, both sets of parents, the coordinator, photographer, DJ or band, and caterer. That's the point of a printable one - print eight copies and nobody texts you 'what time again?' on the morning.

How much buffer time should I build in?

The photographers' rule: add 15 minutes of slack to every block before the ceremony, and never schedule anything in the 30 minutes before guests are seated. Days run late in the morning and catch up at dinner - the buffer belongs up front.

Can I change the timeline's colors?

Yes - five palettes (classic black, sage, blush, navy and gold, terracotta) restyle the printed schedule to match your wedding's look, and print exactly as previewed.

Is the schedule uploaded anywhere?

No - the timeline is assembled by this page's JavaScript in your browser and printed through your browser's own dialog. It keeps working with the internet unplugged.

How these work

A template site normally hands you a DOCX to edit elsewhere, or worse, asks you to type a client's details into their web form. These pages do neither. The form and the document live side by side on one page; JavaScript rebuilds the document on every keystroke; printing goes through your browser's print dialog, which also produces PDFs. Every template ships a "Download blank" button too, for the clipboard-and-pen version.

The same rule that governs FileKiln's converters applies here: the work happens in your browser tab, and nothing you type is transmitted anywhere. Wedding guest lists, construction bids, client contracts, and consent forms with medical disclosures are precisely the documents that should never sit in a stranger's database.

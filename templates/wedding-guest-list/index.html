Wedding Guest List Template

Track invites, RSVPs, and meal choices on one printable guest list with live counts - in your browser, in your palette. Guest names are never uploaded.

Fill in - the document updates live
Preview US Letter, exactly as it prints

Private by construction. The document is assembled by this page's JavaScript as you type. Names, prices, and photos never leave your machine, and the page keeps working with the network unplugged. Printing uses your browser's own print dialog - choose "Save as PDF" there to get a file.

A guest list is the one wedding document that is genuinely sensitive: real names, family sides, who's invited and who isn't. The usual advice is "make a spreadsheet in the cloud" - this page is the alternative that stays on your machine. One line per invitation, and the tracker rebuilds live: party sizes sum into an invited count, RSVPs split into confirmed and awaiting, meals tally for the caterer. Print it, or print the blank version and run the whole campaign in pen.

Questions this template gets asked

How do I use the guest list format?

One line per invitation, as 'Name | party size | side | rsvp | meal'. Only the name is required - 'Rosa & Miguel | 2 | bride | yes | chicken' is a complete entry, and the header counts update live: invited, confirmed, and awaiting reply.

Should I track households or individual guests?

Households (one line per invitation, with a party size) for the invite and RSVP phase - it matches how invitations and reply cards actually work. Switch to per-person detail only for the meal counts your caterer needs, which the meal column carries.

What percentage of invited guests typically attend?

Plan on 75-85% acceptance for a local wedding and 60-75% when significant travel is involved. If your venue caps at 120, you can comfortably invite around 140-150 - and this tracker shows the real number climbing as replies land.

Are my guests' names uploaded anywhere?

No - and for this template that is the whole point. A guest list is a list of real names and family relationships; here it is assembled by JavaScript in your browser, printed through your browser's dialog, and never transmitted.

Can I print a blank list to fill in by hand?

Yes - the Download blank button produces a ruled version with empty rows and checkboxes, ready to print and fill in with a pen.
